Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

In short: GermanBuddy has no account, no server, and no backend. Everything you do in the app — your level, your saved words, your streak, your review schedule — is stored only in a local database on your own device. We (the developer) never receive it, see it, or store a copy of it anywhere.

GermanBuddy ("we", "our", or "the app") is developed by Giacomo Matzeu as a solo developer based in Italy. This privacy policy explains what data the app creates on your device, what — if anything — ever leaves it, and why.

1. There Is No Account

GermanBuddy does not require sign-in and has no user accounts. There is no email address, password, or Google Sign-In involved in using the app. A short on-device "learner profile" (your CEFR level and native language) is created automatically the first time you open the app, tied only to a randomly generated install ID that never leaves your device.

2. Data Stored On Your Device

All app data lives in a local SQLite database and a small amount of local key-value storage on your phone (via expo-sqlite and MMKV). This includes:

  • Learner profile: your CEFR level (A1–C2) and native/UI language
  • Vocabulary and progress: words you've saved, your spaced-repetition (SRS) review schedule, your daily word streak, and your history of daily words
  • Settings: notification preferences and, if you choose to use the optional AI feature described below, your own Anthropic API key

None of this data is transmitted anywhere by default. There is no backend server for GermanBuddy to send it to.

3. Optional AI Feature ("Explore Further")

GermanBuddy includes one optional, opt-in AI feature that generates extra examples and a short usage note for a word you've saved. It is off until you turn it on, and it works differently from a typical app AI feature:

  • You must enter your own Anthropic (Claude) API key in Settings — the app ships with no key of its own
  • When you use the feature, your device calls the Anthropic API directly, with your key — there is no GermanBuddy server in between and nothing is proxied or logged by us
  • Only the word, its level, and your native language are sent to Anthropic to generate the response — never your saved word list, profile, or any other app data
  • Your API key is stored locally on your device only. It is never included in backup exports (see below) and never sent anywhere except directly to Anthropic's API as required to authenticate the request

Use of this feature is subject to Anthropic's own privacy policy, since your device is communicating with Anthropic directly using your own key and your own Anthropic account/usage limits.

4. Local Notifications

If you enable reminders, GermanBuddy schedules local notifications (for your daily word and spaced-repetition reviews) directly on your device using the operating system's notification scheduler. No push token, device identifier, or notification content is sent to us or to any third party — the reminder never leaves your phone.

5. Backup & Restore

You can export a backup of your data as a single .json file from Settings. This is entirely user-initiated: the app hands the file to your device's normal share sheet, and it's up to you where you save it (e.g. Google Drive, Files, email to yourself). We never receive a copy of this file. Your Anthropic API key, if set, is deliberately excluded from backup exports. Restoring a backup replaces the data on your device only.

6. Third Parties

Because the app has no backend, there are very few third parties involved, and none of them are processors of your app data in the traditional sense:

Service What it sees When
Anthropic (Claude API) A single word, CEFR level, and native language — nothing else Only if you set your own API key and use "Explore Further"
Google Play Standard install/crash diagnostics collected by the Play Store platform itself Always (governed by Google's own privacy policy, not ours)
Formspree Your name/email and message, only if you submit the contact form on this support site Only if you contact us — this is our support website, not the app

We do not use Supabase, Firebase, Railway, Upstash, or Sentry — earlier drafts of this policy referenced a cloud backend that GermanBuddy no longer has.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent any information. There is no advertising or analytics SDK in the app, and no data collection to share in the first place — your app data simply stays on your device.

8. Deleting Your Data

Since your data lives only on your device, you are always in full control of it. See the Delete Your Data page for how to remove it — uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your phone's settings, permanently deletes everything immediately. There is nothing stored on a server for us to delete on your behalf.

9. Children's Privacy

GermanBuddy is designed for adult learners aged 18 and above and is not directed at children. Since the app collects no personal information from anyone, there is no data from children under 13 for us to hold or delete.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time — for example, as new opt-in features are added. We will note material changes here with an updated "last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
