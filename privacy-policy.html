Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

GermanBuddy ("we", "our", or "the app") is developed and operated by Giacomo Matzeu as a solo developer based in Italy. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the GermanBuddy mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we collect your email address. This is required to create your account and sync your progress across sessions.

Learner Profile: Your CEFR level (A1–C2), topic preferences, tutor persona settings, and grammar weakness tracking. This data drives which word you receive each day and how your tutor session is built.

Vocabulary and Progress: Words you save to your vault, your spaced-repetition review schedule, your daily word streak, and session statistics (message counts, new words learned, fluency scores).

Chat Session Content: The messages you send during a tutoring session with Lena are processed in real time to generate the tutor's replies and to detect grammar errors. Only the specific error fragments (the incorrect text and its correction) are stored against your grammar profile — we do not store full chat transcripts. The system prompt used for a session (containing your profile snapshot, not your messages) is retained for debugging.

Push Notification Token: A device token used to deliver your daily word reminder.

Usage Data: We may collect crash logs and basic app interaction data to improve the app experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide and improve the GermanBuddy app:

  • To authenticate your account and sync your progress across devices
  • To generate a personalised daily word based on your level, interests, and weak grammar points
  • To power your tutoring conversations and classify grammar errors so we can track your mastery of German grammar rules
  • To schedule vocabulary reviews using spaced repetition
  • To send you a daily push notification when your word is ready
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We use trusted third-party services to provide app functionality. These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

Service Data Processed Purpose
Supabase Account data, learner profile, vocabulary vault, session and grammar records Backend database (PostgreSQL) and storage
Anthropic (Claude API) Chat messages (processed in real time, not persisted as transcripts), profile snapshot used to build prompts Generates your daily word, powers tutor conversations, classifies grammar errors

Railway N/A (infrastructure only) Hosts our backend server and nightly word-generation job

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We do not share your data for advertising or marketing purposes. Data is only sent to the service providers listed above to deliver app functionality.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers. All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your information from unauthorized access. Chat message content is never logged beyond the specific grammar error fragments described above.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, all your data — including your learner profile, vocabulary vault, session history, and grammar tracking — will be permanently removed within 30 days. Certain data may be retained in encrypted backups for up to 90 days after deletion.

7. Your Rights

Under applicable laws, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent for optional data collection

To exercise any of these rights, contact us or use the account deletion form.

8. Children's Privacy

GermanBuddy is designed for adult learners aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will promptly delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy in the app or via email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
