Delete Your Data

GermanBuddy has no account and no server — everything the app knows about your learning (your level, saved words, streak, and review schedule) lives only in a local database on your own device. There is nothing stored anywhere else, so there is no remote "account" for us to delete on your behalf, and no waiting period.

What's stored on your device:

  • Your learner profile (CEFR level, native/UI language)
  • Your saved words and spaced-repetition (SRS) progress
  • Your daily word history and streak
  • Your Anthropic API key, only if you set one for the optional AI feature

Delete everything right now

Choose whichever is easiest — both remove all app data immediately and permanently.

Simplest
  1. Long-press the GermanBuddy icon on your home screen or app drawer
  2. Tap Uninstall
Keep the app, wipe the data
  1. Open your phone's Settings → Apps
  2. Select GermanBuddy
  3. Tap Storage & cache → Clear storage

Either action deletes the app's local database and settings straight away — there's no server-side copy left behind, and nothing for us to process a request for.

A note on backups you made yourself

If you ever used the in-app "Export backup" feature, that created a .json file you chose to save somewhere (e.g. Google Drive, your phone's Files app, or emailed to yourself). Those copies are under your control — deleting them is up to you, wherever you put them. We never receive or keep a copy of your backup file.
